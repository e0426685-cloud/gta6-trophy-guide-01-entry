⚠ One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs it — so do it anyway. There is no mission replay: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.

How this guide is built. Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden). Every guide is written in our own words and names its source beside it — the official description, the PowerPyx trophy guide and roadmap, or the GTABase trophy guide. Steps marked UNCONFIRMED are launch-day reports, not verified walkthroughs: the game launched 19 November 2026, so exact steps for a few trophies are still being confirmed. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving.

Your ticks and spoiler setting are saved on this device only (local storage). Export downloads them as a file; import reads that file back. Full source links are listed in the repository README.
